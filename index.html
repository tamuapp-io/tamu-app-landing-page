What's inside

Everything your hosts need.
Nothing they don’t.

Three simple ideas, executed without compromise.

WhatsApp Recovery

Capture 2 AM revenue.

Turn missed messages into confirmed seats. Most guests book when they have free time — often late at night when your staff is home. Instead of waking up to a mess of unread WhatsApp threads and lost leads, Tamu acts as your 24/7 sales agent, capturing, confirming, and logging every inquiry instantly. Stop losing thousands of dollars every month to the “I’ll reply tomorrow” trap.

No-Show Protection

Secure your margins.

Stop cooking for empty chairs. A no-show isn’t just a missed booking; it’s wasted labor and ingredients. Automated WhatsApp reminders 24h and 1h before keep your brand top-of-mind and force a commitment. If a guest can’t make it, Tamu prompts them to cancel and instantly offers the slot to your waitlist. Recover up to 40% of lost revenue from no-shows without lifting a finger.

High-Velocity Turnover

Seat guests 30% faster.

More covers per shift. During peak hours, every minute a table sits empty between parties is burning cash. With a 15-second walk-in flow and real-time floor synchronization, your hosts move with surgical precision. Faster seating means more table rotations per night — if you can turn just two extra tables per weekend, the system pays for itself ten times over.

Live service

Run a busy Friday from one screen.

Today’s timeline on the left, the floor plan on the right, real-time updates throughout. Your hosts seat, move, and check in guests in two taps — without scrolling, refreshing, or remembering anything.

  • Color-coded reservation states across the entire timeline
  • Walk-ins captured in 3 taps with table suggestions
  • ⌘K to find any guest by name, phone, or confirmation code
  • WebSocket-powered — new bookings appear instantly
