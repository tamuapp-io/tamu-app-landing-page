Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tamu App (operated by Tamu Creative) collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use our website, reservation platform, and WhatsApp messaging services.

1. Who we are

Tamu App provides reservation and guest-management software for restaurants and cafés in Southeast Asia. For privacy-related questions, contact us at:

  • Email: agie@tamucreative.co
  • WhatsApp: +628133763736

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with Tamu, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number (including WhatsApp number).
  • Reservation data — booking date and time, party size, special requests, restaurant visited, and visit history.
  • Account data — login credentials and staff profile information for restaurant users.
  • Communications — messages you send or receive through Tamu, including WhatsApp replies such as confirmations or cancellation requests.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, and usage logs when you visit our website or use our services.

3. WhatsApp and Meta WhatsApp Business API

Tamu uses the Meta WhatsApp Business API (also known as the WhatsApp Cloud API) to send and receive business messages on behalf of restaurants using our platform. This section describes how that integration works and how your data is handled.

3.1 What we use WhatsApp for

  • Reservation confirmations and updates
  • Booking reminders (for example, 24 hours and 1 hour before a reservation)
  • Waitlist notifications and slot offers
  • Responses to guest inquiries initiated via WhatsApp
  • Service-related messages requested by the restaurant you booked with

3.2 Information shared with Meta

To deliver WhatsApp messages, we share the following with Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates as required to operate the WhatsApp Business API:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Message content (including template text and your replies)
  • Message delivery and read status
  • Metadata associated with message delivery (such as timestamps)

Meta processes this information in accordance with its own policies. You can review Meta’s privacy practices at WhatsApp Privacy Policy and Meta Privacy Policy.

3.3 Your consent to WhatsApp messaging

By providing your phone number, completing a reservation, messaging us on WhatsApp, or opting in through a restaurant’s booking flow, you consent to receive service-related WhatsApp messages from Tamu or the restaurant you booked with, sent via the WhatsApp Business API.

Marketing or promotional messages are only sent where permitted by applicable law and where you have given appropriate consent. You may opt out of non-essential messages at any time by replying STOP or contacting us at agie@tamucreative.co.

3.4 Message retention

WhatsApp message content and delivery logs may be stored in Tamu’s systems for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and improve our platform. Meta may retain data according to its own retention schedules.

4. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Process and manage restaurant reservations
  • Send confirmations, reminders, and service notifications (including via WhatsApp and email)
  • Route guest replies to the correct reservation and restaurant
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Improve, secure, and operate our website and platform
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Restaurants you book with — reservation and contact details needed to honour your booking.
  • Meta / WhatsApp — as described in Section 3, to send and receive messages through the WhatsApp Business API.
  • Service providers — hosting, email delivery, payment processors, and analytics providers that help us operate Tamu, under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal authorities — when required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Data storage and security

We store data on secure servers and apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where data is processed outside Indonesia, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place consistent with applicable data-protection requirements.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including providing our services, meeting legal and accounting requirements, and resolving disputes. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal exceptions
  • Withdraw consent for WhatsApp or marketing communications
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact agie@tamucreative.co. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain basic functionality and understand how visitors use our pages. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

10. Children’s privacy

Tamu is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated via our website or WhatsApp where appropriate.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Tamu handles your data in connection with the WhatsApp Business API, please contact:
